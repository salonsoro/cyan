# Contributing to Cyan

**Cyan is not currently accepting external contributions.**

Cyan is independently developed and maintained. All development decisions, architectural choices, implementation details, and changes to the official codebase remain under the exclusive direction of its maintainer.

### External Contributions

The following are not accepted:

- Pull requests.
- Code submissions.
- Patches and proposed modifications to the official codebase.
- External implementations intended for direct integration into Cyan.

Submitted pull requests may be closed without review or further explanation.

### Community Participation

Although external code contributions are not accepted, users are welcome to:

- Report bugs and compatibility issues.
- Submit feature requests and suggestions.
- Participate in discussions.
- Ask questions and share feedback.

Feedback is appreciated but does not imply any obligation to implement suggested changes.

### Independent Development

Users are welcome to fork Cyan and maintain independent modifications, subject to the terms and conditions of the project's license.

Such modifications are not considered official Cyan releases and must not be represented as endorsed or maintained by the Cyan project.

---

Thank you for your interest in Cyan and for respecting its development policy.
